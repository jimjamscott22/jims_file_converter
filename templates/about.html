Bio

Small, made-up bio for now.

Jim is a fictional late-night builder who likes sturdy desktop tools, spare interfaces, and seeing files make it from one side of a problem to the other without drama.

He supposedly started this converter after one too many moments of bouncing between mismatched formats, oversized exports, and services that made a two-second task feel heavier than it needed to be.

The result is a compact workspace built around one idea: drop a file in, convert it cleanly, and move on. No dashboards. No noisy setup. Just a dependable little utility with a colder, more deliberate finish.
